STEP 6 — KPI FORMULAS
============================================================

Total Sales:

=SUM(SalesData[Total_Sales])


Total Profit:

=SUM(SalesData[Profit])


Total Quantity:

=SUM(SalesData[Quantity])


Total Orders:

=COUNTA(UNIQUE(SalesData[Order_ID]))


Total Customers:

=COUNTA(UNIQUE(SalesData[Customer_Name]))


Total Products:

=COUNTA(UNIQUE(SalesData[Product_Name]))


Average Order Value:

=SUM(SalesData[Total_Sales])/
COUNTA(UNIQUE(SalesData[Order_ID]))


Profit Margin:

=SUM(SalesData[Profit])/
SUM(SalesData[Total_Sales])*100
